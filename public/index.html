🎯 Project Objectives & Strategic Alignment

  • Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM): Ensure procured AI software natively integrates with ABHA identifiers, Health Facility/Professional Registries (HFR/HPR), and FHIR R4 exchange protocols.
  • Strategy for AI in Healthcare for India (SAHI - NITI Aayog): Operationalize ethical principles into quantifiable tender due diligence, addressing demographic equity, algorithmic bias, and clinical validation.
  • General Financial Rules (GFR 2017 & GeM): Shift from rudimentary lowest-cost (L1) bidding to Quality and Cost-Based Selection (QCBS 80:20) with mandatory pre-tender sandboxed bench testing.
  • Statutory Safety: Mandate CDSCO Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) licensing under Medical Device Rules 2017, eliminating unapproved software in public clinics.
