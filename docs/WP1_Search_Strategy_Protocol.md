# Work Package 1 (WP1): Structured Search Strategy & Inception Protocol
## Project: AI for Health Procurement Project (KCDH-Ashoka)
**Alignment:** Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM), Strategy for AI in Healthcare for India (SAHI), General Financial Rules (GFR 2017), GeM, CDSCO Medical Device Rules (2017).

---

### 1. Inception & Scope
Public healthcare procurement in India operates under strict administrative, statutory, and fiduciary governance frameworks:
- **General Financial Rules (GFR 2017)**: Rule 144 (Fundamental principles of public buying), Rule 149 (Government e-Marketplace - GeM), Rule 173 (Transparency, competition, fairness), Rule 192 (Quality and Cost Based Selection - QCBS).
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** & ABDM Health Data Management Policy: Strict consent architecture, data fiduciary obligations, and prohibition of unauthorized processing.
- **CDSCO Medical Device Rules (MDR 2017)** & Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) classifications (Class A to D).
- **Public Health Needs**: Low-and-middle-income country (LMIC) infrastructure realities, intermittent connectivity at Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (AAMs), demographic and linguistic heterogeneity.

The objective of WP1 is to establish a rigorous, repeatable, and PRISMA-ScR (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses extension for Scoping Reviews) compliant evidence synthesis protocol.

---

### 2. Conceptual Framework: The PCC / PICO Mapping
- **Population / Context (P)**: Public health procurement authorities in India (Central MoHFW, State Health Societies / NHM, District Health Authorities, Municipal Corporations, State Medical Services Corporations e.g., KMSCL, BMSICL, TNMSC, RMSCL) and comparable international public health procurement bodies (NHS UK, US VA, WHO, European Commission).
- **Intervention / Concept (C)**: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML) based clinical decision-support systems (CDSS), automated screening/diagnostic tools, triage algorithms, and health workflow optimization solutions.
- **Comparison / Focus (C)**: Conventional medical device / IT procurement guidelines versus dedicated AI-for-health procurement due diligence frameworks.
- **Outcomes (O)**: Practical procurement decision pathways, technical evaluation criteria, regulatory validation benchmarks, contractual safeguard clauses, and post-market surveillance protocols.

### 4. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

| Criteria | Inclusion Criteria | Exclusion Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Document Type** | Peer-reviewed studies, government procurement manuals, statutory regulations, multilateral policy frameworks, tender case studies, technical whitepapers. | Opinion editorials without empirical or regulatory grounding, marketing brochures, non-peer reviewed commercial press releases. |
| **Technology Scope** | Clinical AI/ML, diagnostic software (SaMD), automated triage, computer-aided detection (CAD), predictive risk stratification. | General non-AI hardware (e.g., standard monitors), basic billing/accounting ERP with no algorithmic component. |
| **Domain Scope** | Procurement stages: pre-tender feasibility, RFP drafting, technical evaluation, validation, contracting, SLAs, post-procurement monitoring. | Purely theoretical mathematical algorithm proofs with zero implementation or procurement translation. |
| **Language & Period** | English and Hindi official documents; publication period 2017–2026 (coinciding with GFR 2017, ABDM rollout, and global AI governance evolution). | Non-English/non-Hindi documents without certified translation; pre-2015 frameworks lacking modern deep-learning context. |

### 5. Data Extraction & Evidence Mapping Protocol
All extracted evidence will be indexed across 8 standardized analytical domains:
1. *Existing Procurement Principles & Practical Tools*
2. *Clinical & Technical Performance Validation*
3. *Safety, Fairness, Equity & Human Oversight*
4. *Data Governance, Privacy (DPDP) & Cybersecurity*
5. *Interoperability & National Standards (ABDM, FHIR)*
6. *Transparency, Explainability & Auditability*
7. *Regulatory, Legal & Institutional Safeguards (CDSCO, GFR)*
8. *Implementation Readiness, Contracting, SLAs & Post-Market Surveillance*

Each extracted record feeds directly into the **Computational Procurement Resource** database to enable real-time traceability from tender clauses back to verified legal and scientific citations.
